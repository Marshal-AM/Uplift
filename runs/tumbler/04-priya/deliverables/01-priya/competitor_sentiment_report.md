## How to win
- **listing claim** (love:insulation): Lead with supported claim: double-wall vacuum keeps coffee hot 6 hours and cold drinks cold 12 hours, with ceramic interior that does not change coffee taste.
- **proof image** (love:leaks): Show an upside-down sealed tumbler photo plus a short car-cup-holder clip proving the bamboo slide lid stays closed.
- **listing claim** (love:size): State 400 ml capacity and that it fits most car cup holders, aimed at single-serve commute coffee.
- **ad angle** (love:price/value): Position TT-400 at $26 as the commute tumbler that delivers multi-hour heat without Yeti pricing.
- **FAQ** (complaint:cleaning): FAQ: hand-wash only; explain how to slide and rinse the bamboo closure, and warn against dishwasher use that damages coatings on competitors.
- **product improvement** (complaint:lid): Make the bamboo slide removable without tools so the seal can be cleaned, addressing trapped-residue complaints.
- **product improvement** (complaint:flow): Add a small vent path in the slide so liquid pours without the trickle users report on sealed slider lids.
- **offer/guarantee** (complaint:durability): Offer a finish-and-handle warranty covering peeling and breakage, since coating and handle failures drive returns on competitors.
- **proof image** (complaint:handle_durability): Publish a drop/handle stress photo of the travel handle, since broken handles are a distinct complaint on handled tumblers.
- **listing claim** (wish:insulation): Call out the 6-hour hot / 12-hour cold spec next to the ceramic-coated interior so longer hold is a concrete claim, not a wish.
