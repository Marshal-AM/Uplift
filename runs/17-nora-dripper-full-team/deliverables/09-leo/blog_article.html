Are #2 Coffee Filters Bigger Than #4?

No, #2 coffee filters are smaller than #4. Standard cone filters: #1 fits small drippers (1 cup), #2 is for 1-4 cups (common for 02 drippers), and #4 is larger for bigger brewers. For the Dripper D-02, use #2 filters to match its 1-2 cup capacity perfectly. Using the right size prevents overflow and ensures proper flow.

Hario V60 users often choose 02 filters for their 02 drippers, which are equivalent to standard #2 cones but with a specific shape for the V60's design.

Ceramic drippers generally outperform glass for everyday use. The Dripper D-02's ceramic body retains heat better than glass, which can cool too quickly and lead to under-extracted coffee. Glass is transparent and easy to clean but fragile and less insulating.
